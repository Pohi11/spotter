# Design

## Context

The retrospective is a decision record, [0021](../../../../docs/decisions/0021-retrospective-off-the-shelf-alternatives.md), not code. It draws on the measured results in `benchmarks/` and on decision records 0009, 0013, 0014, 0016, and 0019.

## Decisions

### D1. Thresholds, not verdicts
For each alternative the record names what would make it worth adopting (the model's own time dominating a job, many people comparing training runs, event volume or retention beyond JetStream's, a promotion process with more steps and waits than a state machine expresses), so the comparison stays useful as the platform changes.
